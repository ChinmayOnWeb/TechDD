## Review method and limitations

Each SEC filing linked above was retrieved from its accession-specific Archives directory and inspected independently for (1) the balance-sheet date actually presented, (2) the complete liabilities presentation, (3) facility/loan/note disclosures, and (4) contradictory borrowing or unresolved finance-lease evidence. Dates appearing only in comparative income statements, cash-flow statements, or later narrative were not treated as balance-sheet coverage. No CompanyFacts-tag absence was used as evidence of zero.

`VALID_EXACT_DATE` is used rather than `VALID_EXPLICIT_PERIOD_COVERAGE` because every kept candidate is directly presented as a balance-sheet date in its cited filing. No source was inaccessible, so there are no `UNRESOLVED_SOURCE_ACCESS` records. The review is intentionally conservative: the seven contemporaneous affirmative loan contradictions and four wrong-period mappings are removed even though the current extractor may not surface those instruments.
